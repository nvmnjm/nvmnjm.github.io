Profile

Najam Gohar is a physician-researcher and Postdoctoral Fellow at Yale School of Medicine with experience in cardiovascular outcomes research, clinical epidemiology, real-world evidence, large-scale health data, multi-omics, and scientific writing. He uses Python, R, SQL, and statistical software across All of Us, Truveta, TriNetX, UK Biobank, HCUP NIS, and CDC WONDER.

Research interests

  • Cardiovascular and cardiometabolic outcomes
  • Metabolic liver disease
  • Predictive modeling
  • Multimodal clinical data
  • Genomics, transcriptomics, and proteomics
  • Evidence synthesis
